# Geographic evidence reviewed November 25, 2026

The crosswalk has two separate properties: a deterministic source-name link and independent evidence of common geography. The original normalized-name Senate sample contains 689 precincts. Identical names alone never receive geographic verification.

## Accepted evidence and exclusions

- [Beaverhead commissioners, January 12, 2026, Resolution 2026-04](https://beaverheadcountymt.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CommMinutes_20260112.pdf), PDF pages 2–3: precincts 1, 6 and 14 expand with annexations; precincts 2 and 3 contract. These five source-number pairs are marked `boundary_changed`. The resolution explicitly says precinct 12 remains consistent with Ward 1. Its same-number record is accepted as `verified_rename`. The legal-description exhibit is held at the clerk’s office and is not present in the online minutes. Do not infer continuity for the other Beaverhead precincts from this resolution.
- [Lake County election maps](https://www.lakemt.gov/179/Lake-County-Election-Maps) links the [countywide precinct map exported May 19, 2026](https://www.lakemt.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4530/Countywide-Precincts-and-Wards-24x36-20260519). The map explicitly states the precinct period is 2024–2032. Its 22 precinct labels correspond to both result files. [County 2026 election-judge training](https://www.lakemt.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4300/2026-Basic-Election-Judge-Training), PDF page 3, confirms the legislative assignments used to disambiguate source identifiers. These 22 pairs receive `verified_geography`. This is documentary verification based on the stated temporal period, not an overlay of two independently dated polygon files. The complete map rendering is retained as [lake-precincts-20260519.png](lake-precincts-20260519.png); the downloaded PDF’s SHA256 and URL are recorded in geographic-reviews.json. This evidence is scoped to this retained election pair, not a guarantee against future changes.

## Review procedure

Each accepted or rejected pair is explicit in geographic-reviews.json: county, both original precinct identifiers, match status, evidence URL and notes. Reviews fail closed if an identifier disappears or would overwrite another pair. Never infer split/merge weights from votes. A common-geography aggregation would need explicit membership and boundary evidence, a separate aggregate calculation, and its own claims; `verified_aggregation` is deliberately not implemented as a one-to-one winner transition.

To expand geographic coverage, inspect authoritative dated maps, change resolutions or an election-administrator crosswalk. Record what establishes the **same territory in both elections**, then add an explicit pair. Mere polling-place movement, similar names, precinct numbers or equal vote totals are not sufficient by themselves.
